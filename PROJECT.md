## Strategy

Forge serves three purposes: (1) builds Zack's PE deal analysis fluency through structured reps, (2) demonstrates technical ability as a portfolio piece, (3) functions as a demo asset in PE conversations. It's not a commercial product, but it's a credibility builder. Active Tier 2 build.

- 9 realistic company scenarios with escalating difficulty
- Commit-first analysis flow
- LLM-powered feedback on analysis quality
- Persistent scoring and progression
- Value creation bridge research
